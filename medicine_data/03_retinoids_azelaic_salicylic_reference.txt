TOPICAL RETINOIDS (e.g., adapalene, tretinoin): Used in acne care, particularly where blocked pores are involved. May cause dryness, redness, peeling, and irritation. Pregnancy or planning pregnancy is an important exclusion: do not use topical retinoids in pregnancy. Prescription/access status varies by location and product. Professional assessment needed.

AZELAIC ACID: A topical acne treatment that also has roles in some other dermatologist-managed skin conditions. Side effects can include temporary stinging, burning, itching, dry skin and redness. Product strengths, indications and access vary; refer to the specific product label and clinician/pharmacist.

SALICYLIC ACID: An exfoliating ingredient present in certain acne products. It can help with blocked pores; overuse or layering with other actives can irritate skin. It is not a universal solution for all facial bumps or rashes.

PREGNANCY AND OTHER CONTEXT: Do not automatically suggest treatment for a pregnant user, someone planning pregnancy, or a child based on face images. Medication allergies, other treatment use, severity, and current product label matter.

PRESCRIBING: No concentrations, frequencies, duration, or individualized dosing instructions are supplied by this draft.

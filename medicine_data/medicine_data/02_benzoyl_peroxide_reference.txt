ACTIVE INGREDIENT: Benzoyl peroxide.
CLASS: Topical acne therapy; available in gels, washes and sometimes combinations depending on location.
INDICATION CONTEXT: Used for acne, including inflamed spots and blocked pores. A clinician or pharmacist should confirm suitability.
LIMITATIONS: Visible bumps are not proof of acne. Do not recommend benzoyl peroxide solely on the basis of camera images.
POTENTIAL ADVERSE EFFECTS: Dryness, peeling, irritation, mild burning or stinging. Serious allergic symptoms need urgent care.
IMPORTANT PRECAUTIONS: Avoid eyes, mouth, cuts and damaged/infected skin; may bleach fabric/hair. Follow labeled instructions and seek pharmacist/clinician advice when unsure. Sensitivity to sunlight may increase; use sun protection.
INTERACTION/CONTEXT CHECKS: Ask about existing topical acne products and skin irritation. Do not combine multiple irritating ingredients without an appropriate treatment plan.
PATIENT-SPECIFIC DOSAGE: NOT PROVIDED. Product form, strength, labeling and individual considerations require professional review.
WHEN TO ESCALATE: Persistent or worsening acne; marked irritation; signs of infection; new or worsening severe swelling or breathing difficulty (emergency).
RAG POLICY: May describe benzoyl peroxide as a treatment *used for diagnosed acne* with precautions; must not recommend a particular brand, regimen or strength from an image alone.
